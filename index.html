Adaptive computer-based entrance examination conducted by Kathmandu University, Dhulikhel for admission to undergraduate programmes.

Read Before You Begin

Examination Instructions

01

Duration & Structure

Total duration of 120 minutes for 120 multiple-choice questions, distributed equally across three subjects (40 questions per section).

02

Linear Progression

Questions are presented sequentially and cannot be revisited. Once submitted, a response is final and cannot be changed.

03

Adaptive Scoring

The system adjusts question difficulty (Tier 1 to Tier 5) based on your performance. Each correct answer awards 11 to 19 points proportional to difficulty.

04

No Negative Marking

Incorrect responses receive zero marks. However, they lower the difficulty tier and limit your maximum achievable score.

Important

Do not refresh, close, or navigate away from this window during the examination. Doing so will result in automatic submission of your current responses.
